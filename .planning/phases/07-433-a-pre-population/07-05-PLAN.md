## Phase Goal

**As a** ValorTax settlement officer, **I want to** review a 433-A worksheet pre-filled from connected bank, payroll, intake, and Logics data with IRS standards for the client's county, **so that** I only confirm values and read the recommendation instead of retyping the form.

<objective>
Build the proposals pipeline — the `financials_propose` queue job, its triggers, and the first two sources: the address (county from FIPS, ADDR-02) and Logics (marital status, age, liability). Per D-07, D-09, D-12, open questions 4-5.

Purpose: the county is what makes the standards lookup correct, and it must come only from an address (no dropdown). This plan also creates the job and helpers the bank/payroll proposers (Plan 07) and intake (Plan 08) plug into.
Output: `financials/propose/{run,window,cents,address,logics}.ts`, `financials/county.ts`, job dispatch, refresh route, county/employers blocks in GET financials, address-confirm trigger, fake Logics CaseInfo extras, tests, docs.
</objective>
