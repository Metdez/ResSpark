## Phase Goal

**As a** ValorTax settlement officer, **I want to** review a 433-A worksheet pre-filled from connected bank, payroll, intake, and Logics data with IRS standards for the client's county, **so that** I only confirm values and read the recommendation instead of retyping the form.

<objective>
Build the staff 433-A view: every line with its value, status, sources and competing proposals; one-click confirm, edit with a note, Known $0, Unknown and Not applicable; the county state and employers; and the sticky rail with the engine's recommendation and its math, Run evaluation, and Export worksheet. Per D-06, D-10, D-12, D-14, D-15 (UI-SPEC conventions from Phase 1).

Purpose: FORM-03/04/05 and ADDR-02 from the staff side — "see the math", and nothing becomes a fact until staff accept it. The evidence Sheet (Plan 10) and Extract values (Plan 12) attach to these components.
Output: the route, `features/financials/*`, the request-page link, web tests, docs.
</objective>
