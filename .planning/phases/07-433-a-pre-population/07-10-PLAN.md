## Phase Goal

**As a** ValorTax settlement officer, **I want to** review a 433-A worksheet pre-filled from connected bank, payroll, intake, and Logics data with IRS standards for the client's county, **so that** I only confirm values and read the recommendation instead of retyping the form.

<objective>
Let staff click any figure and see exactly where it came from: the transactions by month behind a bank average, the pay stubs behind a wage, the intake answer, the Logics field, the address, or the staff entry. Per D-09, D-10.

Purpose: FORM-03's drill-down — the 433-A is signed under penalty of perjury, so every number shows its evidence before staff accept it.
Output: `financials/evidence.ts`, the evidence route, the SPA Sheet and its wiring, unit/live/web tests, docs.
</objective>
