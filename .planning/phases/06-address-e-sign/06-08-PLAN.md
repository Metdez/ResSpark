## Phase Goal

**As a** tax-resolution client, **I want to** confirm my address and sign my forms on my phone from the same link, **so that** I am done without zooming, scrolling, or waiting on an email.

<objective>
Close Phase 6 on the public URL: a `--esign-flow` smoke that drives the address and signing routes on the deployed Worker in whichever mode the secrets allow, the full gate (`check`, `test:live`, `test:e2e`), an opt-in DocuSeal Test Mode contract check, the operator documents (demo script, USER ACTION list, Test Mode runbook, real-device checklist), and the time-boxed Anvil comparison D-09 asks for.

Purpose: the demo-safety bar — with or without vendor keys, both steps behave honestly on the deployed sandbox — and the held-out human checks no fixture can replace.
Output: `smoke.mjs --esign-flow`, `smoke:esign` script, `docuseal-contract.test.ts`, README Phase 6 sections, two runbooks, green gates, the Anvil notes in the SUMMARY.
</objective>
