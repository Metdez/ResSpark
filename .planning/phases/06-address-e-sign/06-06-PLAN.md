## Phase Goal

**As a** tax-resolution client, **I want to** confirm my address and sign my forms on my phone from the same link, **so that** I am done without zooming, scrolling, or waiting on an email.

<objective>
Make signing on a phone pass the "senior on the phone" test: the signature row's `Sign` opens a full-bleed signing route that asks for ESIGN consent once and then shows DocuSeal's embedded form with the signature field active; a copied signing link deep-links straight there after `Start`; the portal CSP admits DocuSeal's script, fetches, and font. Prove the tap budget with Playwright at 390×844.

Purpose: SIGN-04 ("at most 3 taps with no pinch-zoom or horizontal scrolling, and the agent can see which step the client is on") and SIGN-03's client half (consent before signing, the copyable deep link).
Output: `features/client-portal/sign/*`, `routes/c/sign.$itemId.tsx`, the `Sign` row and deep link, the `points` list on the consent sheet, `client-link.ts` `step` forwarding, CSP hosts, Playwright config and spec, `@docuseal/react` and `@playwright/test` pinned, companion docs.
</objective>
