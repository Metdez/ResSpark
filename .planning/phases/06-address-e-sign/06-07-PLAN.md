## Phase Goal

**As a** tax-resolution client, **I want to** confirm my address and sign my forms on my phone from the same link, **so that** I am done without zooming, scrolling, or waiting on an email.

<objective>
Give staff the signature card D-20 describes: template name, an honest status chip, the `Test` badge, a copyable (and emailable) signing link that is always the ResSpark link plus `?step=`, Refresh, the signed and audit PDFs as normal document rows, and the IRS online-submission note for 2848/8821 — plus timeline labels for every e-sign event.

Purpose: SIGN-03's "staff get a copyable signing link (not just an email)", SIGN-02's "the item shows as signed", and SIGN-04's "the agent can see which step the client is on".
Output: `db/signature-view.ts`, `signatures` on the review response, `review/{signature-card,signature-status}.tsx|ts`, the `step` option on the Phase 2 email route, timeline labels, tests, companion docs.
</objective>
