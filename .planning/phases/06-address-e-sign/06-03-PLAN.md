## Phase Goal

**As a** tax-resolution client, **I want to** confirm my address and sign my forms on my phone from the same link, **so that** I am done without zooming, scrolling, or waiting on an email.

<objective>
Make the address step real on both sides: the client types, picks a USPS suggestion (or expands units), confirms, and is done; when Smarty is missing or fails, the manual form takes over; staff see the standardized address, county, FIPS, RDI, verification state, and source in the review panel and approve it like any item.

Purpose: ADDR-01's user-visible half — Andrew's "type in your home address… did you mean this? Yes… you can now skip the whole county".
Output: `features/client-portal/address/*`, the `address` branch in `step-list.tsx`, `address` on the staff review response, `review/address-card.tsx`, the `address.confirmed` timeline label, tests, companion docs.
</objective>
