## Phase Goal

**As a** tax-resolution client, **I want to** confirm my address and sign my forms on my phone from the same link, **so that** I am done without zooming, scrolling, or waiting on an email.

<objective>
Let staff attach an e-sign template to a request: an admin maps each form key to a console-built DocuSeal template on Settings → Integrations, and the request builder gains "Add a form to sign" limited to mapped keys. The Worker enforces the three-key whitelist on both the mapping and request creation.

Purpose: SIGN-01's staff half — "Staff can attach an e-sign template (engagement letter, Form 2848, Form 8821) to a request" — with Form 433-A unreachable by construction.
Output: `routes/admin-esign.ts` mounted, `setEsignMapping`, the settings card and its API client, the builder addition, the `bad_template` gate on request creation, tests, companion docs.
</objective>
