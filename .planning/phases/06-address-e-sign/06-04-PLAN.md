## Phase Goal

**As a** tax-resolution client, **I want to** confirm my address and sign my forms on my phone from the same link, **so that** I am done without zooming, scrolling, or waiting on an email.

<objective>
Close the signing loop on the server: the DocuSeal HMAC webhook adapter on the Phase 4 route, the `signature` branch of the reconciler, and the ingest that lands the signed PDF and the audit-trail PDF in the case with a content hash. Prove it live: a signed `form.completed` → 200 + ledger → consumer → stubbed `GET /submissions/{id}` → two downloads → two `documents` rows with sha256 → connection `done` → item `submitted` → request `waiting_on_staff`; then prove the signed PDF pushes to Logics in fake mode after approval.

Purpose: SIGN-02 — "stored in the case immediately with a content hash, the item is marked signed, and the signed PDF can be pushed to Logics like any other document" — and the DocuSeal clause of WEBH-01.
Output: `webhooks/docuseal.ts`, `jobs/signature-reconcile.ts`, `integrations/esign/ingest.ts`; widened Phase 4 unions; fixtures; signer helper; two live suites and unit tests; companion docs.
</objective>
