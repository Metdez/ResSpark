## Phase Goal

**As a** tax-resolution client, **I want to** connect my payroll and my bank from the link on my phone, **so that** my pay stubs, W-2s, and 6 months of bank statements reach my case manager without me hunting for documents.

<objective>
Deliver the bank vertical on the server behind the `BankProvider` interface: Plaid Hosted Link minting per tap (row before URL), the single-use return route, session completion (webhook or `/link/token/get`) with public-token exchange into the AES-GCM vault, the `/transactions/sync` cursor loop into `bank_transactions`, statement PDFs into Storage with per-account month cells (available / not supported / manual / missing / pending), manual-statement attribution, `ITEM_LOGIN_REQUIRED` handling, and daily Item removal. Prove it live with Plaid stubbed and everything else real.

Purpose: BANK-01..06 are the demo's "6 months of statements appear in the case" promise; the capability-keyed interface is what makes Teller a Phase 8 configuration change (BANK-04).
Output: `integrations/bank/{index,start,plaid/*}.ts`, `integrations/token-vault.ts`, `db/bank.ts`, `jobs/{bank-reconcile,bank-statements,remove-items}.ts`, `routes/c-return.ts`, the bank branches in `c-connections.ts`, `reconcile.ts`, `jobs/index.ts`, `index.ts`; Plaid fixtures; three live tests; companion docs.
</objective>
