## Phase Goal

**As a** tax-resolution client, **I want to** connect my payroll and my bank from the link on my phone, **so that** my pay stubs, W-2s, and 6 months of bank statements reach my case manager without me hunting for documents.

<objective>
Give staff the connection view: a card per connection with an honest status badge, timestamps, and a Refresh button; for banks the accounts × 6-months statement grid with the last-3 / months-4–6 split and a 3/6-month transactions table with money formatting; for payroll the employers with their pay stubs, W-2s, and income report links — all polled live on the request page beside the Phase 3 review panel.

Purpose: Andrew must see, within seconds of the client finishing, that the statements and stubs are in the case and which months need chasing (ROADMAP SC 1 and 3).
Output: `features/review/connections/*`, the request-page mount, tests, companion docs.
</objective>
