## Phase Goal

**As a** tax-resolution client, **I want to** connect my payroll and my bank from the link on my phone, **so that** my pay stubs, W-2s, and 6 months of bank statements reach my case manager without me hunting for documents.

<objective>
Replace the two `Coming next` placeholders with the real connect steps on the phone: consent sheets with the D-12 wording, the payroll sheet that opens Truv Bridge inside the portal and lets the client add several employers, the bank sheet that sends the client to Plaid Hosted Link and then shows each account's 6 months with upload buttons for the months the bank cannot provide, the `/c/return` page that only polls, and the Phase 3 upload sheet reused as the fallback in both steps.

Purpose: this is what Andrew's client sees; every state must be honest (server-derived), reachable at 44 px, and degrade to upload when a provider is not configured.
Output: `features/client-portal/connect/*`, `routes/c/return.tsx`, `step-list.tsx`/`portal-page.tsx` edits, tests, companion docs; build and code-split gates green.
</objective>
