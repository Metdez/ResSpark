## Phase Goal

**As a** tax-resolution client, **I want to** connect my payroll and my bank from the link on my phone, **so that** my pay stubs, W-2s, and 6 months of bank statements reach my case manager without me hunting for documents.

<objective>
Make the framework self-healing and operable: the 15-minute reconciler and daily retention trigger in `scheduled()`, the staff read model and routes (list connections for a request, Refresh, transactions), and the Worker configuration that Phase 4 needs — queue `max_retries: 5` with a DLQ consumer, two crons, provider vars, `TOKEN_ENC_KEY_1`, and stdin-only provisioning of the vendor secrets. Deploy green without any Plaid or Truv credential and prove the webhook route on the public URL.

Purpose: Truv disables failing webhook endpoints without replay and Plaid's redirect fires on exit, so a missed webhook must never leave a request stuck (WEBH-02); staff need one button and one list to see and nudge every connection (D-04).
Output: `cron.ts`, `db/connections-ops.ts`, `routes/admin-connections.ts` mounted and tested; `wrangler.jsonc` + `worker-configuration.d.ts` regenerated; `provision-secrets.mjs --provider`; redeployed Worker with new smoke checks; README secrets and USER ACTION sections.
</objective>
