## Phase Goal

**As a** tax-resolution client, **I want to** connect my payroll and my bank from the link on my phone, **so that** my pay stubs, W-2s, and 6 months of bank statements reach my case manager without me hunting for documents.

<objective>
Deliver the payroll vertical on the server: consent recording, the client connection routes (`GET item connection`, `POST consents`, `POST :kind/start`, `POST :id/reconcile`), Truv order creation with order reuse for multi-employer, the report-and-file fetch that lands pay stubs, W-2s, and the income report in the case attributed to each employer, and the portal CSP that lets Truv Bridge load. Prove it live twice: start (cookie → consent → start → stubbed `POST /v1/orders/` → row + bridge token) and fetch (two completed employers → stubbed reports and PDFs → Storage objects + `documents` rows → `done` → idempotent rerun).

Purpose: Andrew's "two jobs, one 1099" case is the acceptance test — one order, several employers, every file attributed; the ResSpark upload fallback (Plan 05) must sit beside it, not replace it.
Output: `c-connections.ts` mounted under `/api/c` with CSP on `/c/*`; `db/consents.ts`; `consent-text.ts`; `truv/{start,reports}.ts`; `storage/connection-files.ts`; `payroll-reconcile.ts` extended; fixtures; two live tests; companion docs.
</objective>
