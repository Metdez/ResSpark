## Phase Goal

**As a** ValorTax case manager, **I want to** nudge clients automatically, offer a cheaper second bank option, and run on a platform that meets security and vendor requirements, **so that** ResSpark can move from the demo to live ValorTax cases.

<objective>
Implement Teller behind Phase 4's `BankProvider` so the firm can switch bank providers with one setting (REM-03): server-minted nonce at start, verified enrollment at completion, transactions-only sync over the mTLS binding, statement months marked not supported, and removal that ends billing. Built on the spike's real sandbox fixtures, not on guesses.

Purpose: Andrew called Teller "a one-line configuration change… save you a ton of cash" versus Plaid's $10-14 per user. This plan is the Worker half; Plan 10 adds Teller webhooks, Teller Connect in the client's bank step, and the Settings switch.
Output: `integrations/bank/teller/*`, provider resolution from the firm setting, the start-response variant, `teller-complete`, full-window pending cleanup, not-supported cells, the certificate binding, live and unit tests, companion docs.
</objective>
