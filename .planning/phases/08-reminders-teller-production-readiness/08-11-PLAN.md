## Phase Goal

**As a** ValorTax case manager, **I want to** nudge clients automatically, offer a cheaper second bank option, and run on a platform that meets security and vendor requirements, **so that** ResSpark can move from the demo to live ValorTax cases.

<objective>
Produce the WISP evidence pack (PROD-02): a Markdown file generated from live configuration and the audit log, plus a CSV of the full access log, downloadable by an admin from a new Security settings page. Give the firm-level Phase 8 switches a home: the Security page also hosts the retention and AI cards, and a new Client links page holds reminders, stuck-client hours, and the identity-check default.

Purpose: IRS Pub 5708 and 16 CFR 314.4 expect a firm to show its safeguards; generating the evidence from the running system means it never goes stale. The settings pages are where staff "switch it" without touching Wrangler (D-10 spirit, D-01).
Output: `compliance/{evidence,access-csv,static-sections}.ts`, `db/compliance.ts`, `routes/admin-compliance.ts`, the two settings pages and routes, settings nav, live and unit tests, companion docs.
</objective>
