## Phase Goal

**As a** ValorTax case manager, **I want to** nudge clients automatically, offer a cheaper second bank option, and run on a platform that meets security and vendor requirements, **so that** ResSpark can move from the demo to live ValorTax cases.

<objective>
Build the retention purge (PROD-03): two years after a case's last activity (configurable, minimum 30 days), remove its provider connections at the vendor, delete its stored files and connected data, keep documents only as tombstones, strip PII from the rows that remain, and keep the audit record. It ships switched off; Plan 12 holds the only path to turning it on.

Purpose: 16 CFR 314.4(c)(6) caps retention at two years after last use, and Logics is the system of record. The research found D-17 incomplete against the real schema (NOT NULL document columns, PII added by Phases 2, 6, 7, Teller webhook payloads); this plan applies those corrections.
Output: `0011_retention_purge.sql`, `retention/{tick,remove-providers}.ts`, `jobs/purge-case.ts` (replacing Plan 01's stub), Truv link deletion, the cron step, the preview endpoint, the admin retention card, live and unit tests, companion docs.
</objective>
