## Phase Goal

**As a** ValorTax case manager, **I want to** review what clients upload from their phones and watch status update live, **so that** I can approve or reject each item without chasing anyone.

<objective>
Give staff the review loop on the Phase 2 request page: items with their documents, Preview/Download through 5-minute signed URLs, Approve, and Reject-with-note — every decision through the Plan 01 RPCs — refreshed by a 5 s visibility-aware poll. Also expose the two read endpoints Plans 04/05 render (`/review` with `client_session`, `/cases/:id/events`) and the shared `StatusBadge`, `pollInterval`, and `relative-time` helpers.

Purpose: UPL-03 and UPL-04 staff half; STAT-02 for the request view; data for STAT-03/STAT-04.
Output: `db/review.ts`, `routes/admin-review.ts`, `routes/admin-events.ts` (registered), `components/status-badge.tsx`, `lib/polling.ts`, `lib/relative-time.ts`, `features/review/*`, request page wiring, tests, docs.
</objective>
