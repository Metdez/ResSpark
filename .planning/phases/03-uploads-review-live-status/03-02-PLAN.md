## Phase Goal

**As a** ValorTax case manager, **I want to** review what clients upload from their phones and watch status update live, **so that** I can approve or reject each item without chasing anyone.

<objective>

Purpose: UPL-01/UPL-02 client half and the client side of UPL-03 (rejected items return to the step list with the note).
Output: `features/uploads/*` (accept, preprocess, api, transport, copy, file-row, upload-sheet + tests), the Phase 2 step list wired to the sheet, `heic2any` (and conditionally `tus-js-client`) added to `app/package.json`, companion docs.
