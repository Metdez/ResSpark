## Phase Goal

**As a** ValorTax case manager, **I want to** push approved documents into the client's Logics case with one click, **so that** the file is complete in the system of record without downloading and re-uploading by hand.

<objective>
Expand the Plan 01 consumer from "small PDF pass-through" to the full PUSH-02 path: download, hash-check, normalize (split / wrap / flag), store parts under the document's `logics/` prefix with their own hashes, mint sibling rows and messages, then dispatch. Add the cron sweeper and the DLQ rule so a lost consumer never blocks a case or duplicates a send, and give the firm its D-08 configuration (`file_category_id`, `activity_type`, `write_enabled`) with the Test-connection ActivityType listing.

Purpose: after this plan a 20 MB statement becomes four ≤ 5.5 MiB parts in Logics with correct names, a photo becomes a PDF, an encrypted PDF becomes a clear staff instruction — and nothing over the gate is ever sent.
Output: `jobs/logics-push-normalize.ts`, `storage/logics-parts.ts`, `jobs/logics-push-sweep.ts`, `cron.ts` + `jobs/dlq.ts` wiring, settings on `routes/admin-integrations.ts`, consumer unit matrix, live multipart proof added to `logics-push.test.ts`, companion docs.
</objective>

|----------|-------------|
