## Phase Goal

**As a** ValorTax case manager, **I want to** push approved documents into the client's Logics case with one click, **so that** the file is complete in the system of record without downloading and re-uploading by hand.

<objective>
Give staff the one-click push on the request page: a card of approved documents with multi-select and the computed plan, a confirmation sheet that shows exactly what will land in Logics (files, parts, manual-compression flags, the comment, the activity note), inline ledger chips per document that poll, a `Simulated` badge in fake mode, and the two-action resolution dialog for `unknown`. Update the SANDBOX banner for fake mode.

Purpose: PUSH-01's "one click", PUSH-02's "flagged before the click", and PUSH-04's "staff decision in the UI" all live here; this is what Andrew clicks on Oct 12.
Output: `features/logics-push/*` (api, plan, hook, card, sheet, dialog) with jsdom tests, `request-page.tsx` mount, `status-badge.tsx` push tones, banner text, `isConfig.logics_mode`, companion docs.
</objective>
